What happens next

  1. You leave your email address here.
  2. When it's your turn, I email you. Requests are handled in the order they arrive.
  3. You reply with your name, shipping address and how many boards you want, and you pay.
  4. I ship your board and send you the tracking number.

A request is not a reservation. The stock number above is updated by hand, so a board is yours once I've confirmed it by email.

Good to know

Shipping
Ships from Norway. Shipping varies depending on your country and comes on top of the board price, as do any import VAT or duties your country charges.
Payment
PayPal. You pay after I've confirmed your board, not before.
Order
First come, first served.
